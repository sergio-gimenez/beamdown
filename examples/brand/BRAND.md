# Brand guidelines for slides

Example Co. presentations. An agent reads this before drafting a deck, and puts the
colors and type into the deck's front matter.

## Colors

- Background `#ffffff`, text `#1a1a2e`, accent `#e4572e`.
- Muted text and code comments `#6b6b80`.
- Use the accent for one thing per slide: the key number, a highlighted word, or
  the current step.

## Type

- Headings in Inter Display, weight 800; body text in Inter.
- Body text at 30 px, headings at 72 px, so every slide lines up.

## Every deck

- Running header: "Example Co. · <talk title>".
- Open with a title slide and close with one takeaway and a contact line.
- Name each slide's section (Context, Problem, Approach, Results, Next).
- The logo `example-logo.svg` sits bottom right on the first and last slides,
  at 8 %.

## Writing

- One idea per slide. At most four bullets, each under eight words.
- Headlines say the point ("Latency halves at the edge"), not the topic
  ("Latency").
- Prefer a chart or a diagram to a paragraph. Put details in speaker notes.
